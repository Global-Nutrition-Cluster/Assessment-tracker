How to read these figures

Definitions

SMART GAM by WHZ includes oedema and covers 6–59 months. DHS wasting is weight-for-height only (no oedema, no MUAC), 0–59 months. MUAC and combined GAM are separate indicators. DHS values are shown exactly as published; DHS does not publish MAM.

Comparability

Every value shows its source, survey year, area level and age group. SMART mortality (deaths/10,000/day, recall of a few months) and DHS mortality (per 1,000 live births, 5 years) are separate indicators and never share a scale. "Among children reported ill" shares are not prevalences.

Thresholds and age of data

Classes follow WHO 2018 prevalence thresholds for wasting, stunting and overweight. Other indicators use a continuous scale without classes. Surveys older than 3 years are dotted on the map and marked in lists. SMART values are "To review" until validated.

Results come from published survey reports (SMART) and the DHS Program API. Values are shown as published. MICS results will be added as a third source.
