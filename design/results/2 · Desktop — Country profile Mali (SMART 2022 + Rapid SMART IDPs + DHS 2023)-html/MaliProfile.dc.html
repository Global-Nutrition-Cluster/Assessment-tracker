Notes from the data

  • SMART 2022: mortality recall period stated inconsistently in the report — 90 days from 26 March 2022 (methodology), about 108 days (section 4.3).
  • SMART 2022: fever is reported as "fever/malaria with or without convulsions".

  • All SMART values are extracted from the final report dated 28 November 2022 and are still "To review".
How to read these figures. Each source sits in its own block, with its own definition, age group and area level. Mortality is never plotted on a shared axis: SMART reports deaths per 10,000 per day over a recall of a few months, DHS reports deaths per 1,000 live births over 5 years. WHO 2018 classes are used for wasting, stunting and overweight only; other indicators have no classes.
Results come from published survey reports (SMART) and the DHS Program API. Values are shown as published.
